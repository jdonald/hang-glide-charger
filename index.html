How to play ❓

  • 🏃 Charge: Stand on a treadmill — your power ⚡ grows while you run. Better treadmills charge faster, and they keep charging AFK!
  • 🪂 Glide: Go to a launch gate at the cliff edge and tap LAUNCH. Steer with the stick. Dive to go faster, climb to float further.
  • ⚡ Boost: Tap BOOST while gliding to spend 10⚡ on a burst of speed + lift.
  • ⭐ Rings: Fly through golden rings for speed and bonus power.
  • 🏝️ Land on the far island to beat the level. Longer levels need more charge — if you splash, go charge more!
  • 📷 Camera: Drag on the right side of the screen to look around.
